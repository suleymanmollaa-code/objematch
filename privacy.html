Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

ObjeMatch ("we", "us", "our") operates the website objematch.com. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you use our service:

  • Account data: email address, name, and hashed password when you register.
  • Photos: images you upload for analysis. These are processed in memory and not permanently stored.
  • Usage data: number of analyses performed per month to enforce the free tier limit.
  • Watch data: item titles and descriptions you choose to monitor, along with your preferred notification frequency.
  • Payment data: processed by LemonSqueezy. We do not store your card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core service: AI object identification and Amazon/eBay link generation.
  • To send deal alert emails when a Watch finds new results (only if enabled).
  • To enforce the monthly 100 free analysis limit.
  • To process subscription payments via LemonSqueezy.

3. Photos and AI Processing

Uploaded photos are sent to Anthropic's Claude API for AI analysis. Photos are processed in memory and are not stored on our servers beyond the duration of the request. A thumbnail may be saved to your analysis history if you are logged in. You can delete your analysis history from your dashboard at any time.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Anthropic (Claude API): processes your photos for object identification. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy.
  • LemonSqueezy: handles subscription payments. See LemonSqueezy's Privacy Policy.
  • Amazon Associates / eBay: we generate affiliate links. Clicking them may set cookies from those platforms.

5. Data Retention

Your account and analysis history are retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing us.

6. Cookies

We use localStorage to store your authentication token. We do not use tracking cookies. Third-party affiliate links (Amazon, eBay) may set their own cookies when clicked.

7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

8. Children's Privacy

Our service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes via email or a notice on the site.

10. Contact

For privacy-related questions, please contact us via the ObjeMatch website.
