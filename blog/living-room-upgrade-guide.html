10 Things Your Living Room Is Missing (And Where to Find Them)

5 min read · June 2025

Most living rooms aren't bad — they're just unfinished. A couch, a TV, maybe a coffee table. But something feels off. The space doesn't feel cohesive, warm, or intentional. Usually it comes down to a handful of missing items that most people overlook.

Here are the 10 things that make the biggest difference — and exactly what to look for on Amazon or eBay.

1
An Area Rug
Without a rug, furniture floats. A rug anchors your seating area and instantly makes the room feel designed. Size matters: go bigger than you think — at least 8x10 for most living rooms. The front legs of your sofa should sit on it.

2
A Floor Lamp
Overhead lighting makes every room look like a hospital waiting room. A floor lamp in the corner adds ambient light and warmth. Arc floor lamps are particularly good for reading nooks next to a sofa.
Shop Amazon Find Used on eBay
3
Wall Art or a Gallery Wall
Blank walls make a room feel unfinished. You don't need expensive art — a set of matching frames with prints, a large canvas, or even a mirror can transform a bare wall. Above the sofa is the most common missed opportunity.
Shop Amazon Find Used on eBay
4
Throw Pillows and a Blanket
Pillows add texture, color, and comfort. You need at least 2-4 on a standard sofa — mixing sizes and textures works better than matching sets. A throw blanket draped over one arm completes the look.
Shop Amazon
5
A Coffee Table Tray
A coffee table without organization looks messy. A simple tray groups your remotes, candles, and coasters into a contained, intentional-looking arrangement. Takes 30 seconds, makes a big difference.
Shop Amazon
6
An Accent Chair
A sofa alone doesn't create a seating arrangement — it creates a line. An accent chair at an angle, even a small one, makes the space conversational and complete. Velvet accent chairs are a popular choice for color without commitment.
Shop Amazon Find Used on eBay
7
Plants (Real or High-Quality Faux)
Plants add life to a room — literally. A large fiddle leaf fig or monstera in a corner, or a snake plant on a shelf, softens the hard edges of furniture and adds organic texture. If you can't keep plants alive, high-quality faux ones are indistinguishable from a distance.
Shop Amazon
8
Curtains That Actually Fit
Most people hang curtains too low and too short. They should start near the ceiling and fall to the floor — this makes ceilings look higher and windows look larger. Linen or velvet curtains in a neutral tone transform a room more than almost anything else on this list.
Shop Amazon
9
Side Tables
Every seat needs a surface nearby. Without side tables, drinks end up on the floor and remotes disappear. Nesting tables are flexible — use one, stack both when you need the space.
Shop Amazon Find Used on eBay
10
Bookshelf or Display Shelves
A styled bookshelf or floating wall shelves add personality and fill vertical space that most living rooms waste. Books, a plant, a candle, and one decorative object — that's the formula for a styled shelf.
