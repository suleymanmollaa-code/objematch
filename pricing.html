Frequently asked questions
What counts as an "analysis"?
Each time you upload a photo and run the AI, that's one analysis. Free users get 5 per month. The counter resets on the 1st of each month.
What is "weekly deal monitoring"?
Pro users can mark any analyzed photo as "monitored." Our system re-runs the AI every week to find fresh Amazon and eBay deals for the objects in that photo, then emails you a digest.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel at any time from your account settings. You'll keep Pro access until the end of your billing period.
Is the Amazon affiliate program included?
Yes. All Amazon links use the ObjeMatch affiliate tag. You don't need your own affiliate account — just click through and shop normally.
