FAQ
What is a "watch"?
After analyzing a photo, you can click "Watch" on any identified object. Our AI searches Amazon and eBay for that specific item at your chosen frequency and emails you a digest of new deals.
Is the analysis really free?
Yes. You can upload photos and get instant buy/sell links 100 times per month at no cost. Watching is only needed if you want ongoing automated research for a specific item.
Can I watch multiple items?
Yes. Each item is a separate watch. You can mix and match frequencies — watch your sofa daily and your lamp monthly, for example.
How do I cancel a watch?
Go to your dashboard, find the watch under "My Watches," and click Cancel. Your subscription stops immediately and you won't be charged again.
How do I receive deal alerts?
New deals are emailed to your registered address and saved in your dashboard under "My Watches."
